# FinKore Trademark Guidance

Status: Initial trademark guidance - formal trademark policy TBD.

Copyright licensing and trademark rights are separate. The MPL-2.0 license for FinKore source code does not automatically grant trademark rights in the FinKore name, logos, badges, or release branding.

The open-source license does not grant permission to falsely represent modified versions, forks, services, packages, or distributions as official FinKore releases.

This document does not claim that FinKore is a registered trademark and does not create a complete trademark policy. A formal trademark policy remains a future governance task.
