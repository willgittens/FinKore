### LGC-0001 — Preserve the magnitude of a financial value; do not silently truncate it

Status: `reviewed`; interpretation: `analyst_inference`; normative acceptance: `not_requested`; finding: `pending_validation`.

**Analyst interpretation.** The publicly reported numeric-field incident illustrates financial damage from losing significant digits, distinct from deliberate rounding. The connection to FinKore is the analyst's interpretation, not an assertion that the interview established its decimal contract.

**Conditions and limits.** This is a personal recollection, not a verified experiment or a universal statement about COBOL. No details from the withheld follow-up are used. Exact representability does not define universal database field capacity. The Open Questions in spec/money/representation.md leave portable precision, scale and overflow limits unresolved: this behavior is not currently defined by FinKore Core. Unsupported amounts already have a portable failure category; this comparison does not set the capacity threshold or introduce an overflow code.

**Potential Learn benefit.** Explain why silent loss of high-order digits is different from an explicit rounding decision, and ask consumers to document their supported value profile without claiming a universal FinKore maximum digit count.

## Maintenance and limits

See [tool usage](tools/README.md). Only `traceability.json.curation` is editable. The `resolved` section and this report are generated. New interviews require explicit registration and section classification; directory discovery never approves or publishes a draft. Changed interviews, clauses, or related research curation require an explicit semantic recheck. Rebuilding does not refresh reviewed fingerprints.

The shared research contract reader supplies explicit CoreDoc/Spec references, manifest-discovered vectors, published coverage limits, and registry-listed Agentic operations. No lexical similarity or historical implementation creates a requirement. The specification-first process remains necessary for any proposed financial behavior; this index is not a publication gate.
