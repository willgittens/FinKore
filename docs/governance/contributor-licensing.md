# Contributor Licensing

Status: Open governance decision

FinKore community source code is licensed under the Mozilla Public License 2.0 (`MPL-2.0`). Contributions to MPL-2.0-covered portions of the repository are expected to be submitted under the licensing terms applicable to those portions, unless explicitly agreed otherwise in writing.

FinKore does not currently require a Contributor License Agreement, copyright assignment, or similar contributor-rights agreement.

## Governance Issue

External contributors normally retain copyright in their contributions unless another agreement says otherwise. As a project receives meaningful external contributions, future relicensing, alternative distribution, certified distribution, or commercial licensing can become more difficult without a clear contributor-rights mechanism.

FinKore is evaluating whether a future Contributor License Agreement or similar mechanism is appropriate, particularly because the project may eventually support Enterprise editions, commercial licensing, certified distributions, or alternative distributions.

No contributor is required by this document to assign copyright.

## Future Decision Points

- Whether FinKore should adopt a CLA, Developer Certificate of Origin, or another contribution attestation model.
- Whether different repository areas need different contribution terms.
- How any future Enterprise or certified distribution strategy should coexist with community MPL-2.0 rights.
- How copyright ownership and attribution should be recorded.

Nothing in this document is legal advice.
