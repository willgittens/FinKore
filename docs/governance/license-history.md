## Previous Licensing

The project was previously distributed under the MIT License.

Versions or copies of FinKore that were validly distributed under the MIT License remain governed by the license grant applicable to those copies. Changing the license of subsequent repository versions does not revoke permissions already granted for previously distributed MIT-licensed copies.

## Scope of Relicensing

The migration applies to project-owned repository content unless a file states otherwise, including source code, tests, specifications, conformance material, agentic contract documentation, scripts, and project documentation.

Third-party dependencies, generated dependency metadata, and any future vendored third-party code remain under their original licenses and are not relicensed by the FinKore MPL-2.0 declaration.

## Known Exceptions

No project-owned vendored third-party source files or alternate license notices were identified during this migration audit.

Dependency license declarations in `node_modules/` and lockfiles are third-party metadata and remain accurate for those dependencies.

## Unresolved Ownership Questions

Repository history reviewed during this migration shows commits authored by Will Gittens using `will@betridee.com`. This is repository evidence only and is not a definitive legal ownership analysis.

The project should make future decisions about copyright-holder identity, contributor-rights governance, and whether a CLA or similar mechanism is appropriate before accepting significant external contributions.

## Rationale

FinKore intends to become a broadly adopted financial software standard. The project chose MPL-2.0 to balance adoption, contribution, future commercial sustainability, and protection of the open implementation.

This rationale does not add restrictions to MPL-2.0.
