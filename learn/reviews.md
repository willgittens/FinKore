Financial rules become easier to understand when they are connected to the work
of people who investigate discrepancies, authorize adjustments and explain what
happened to customers' funds. Reviews brings those operational accounts together
with explicit comparisons to the FinKore CoreDoc.

Read a specialist's experience, follow the connection to a rule and inspect the
complete interview behind the analysis.

## Why expert consultation matters

FinKore's Expert Review Program connects software engineering with professionals
who understand the financial world beyond the code. Accounting, auditing,
banking, legal, compliance and risk perspectives can reveal distinctions that
a technical implementation alone does not explain.

One account can help explain a control, illustrate an operational risk or expose
a question for further investigation. The reports separate what the specialist
said, the analyst's comparison and the limits of that comparison. They do not
claim that one institution or one professional establishes a universal policy.

## How the Expert Review Program works

1. **Share your expertise.** Answer a question, describe a real-world situation, challenge an assumption or identify a missing financial control.
2. **Review the domain.** Contributions are evaluated against existing FinKore principles, financial context and other expert feedback.
3. **Define the behavior.** Validated findings may lead to CoreDoc clarifications, new specifications, conformance scenarios or documented limitations.
4. **Build with confidence.** Portable specifications help developers implement consistent financial behavior across languages and platforms.

Expert contributions inform FinKore's specification process. Participation does
not automatically constitute normative approval, certification or regulatory endorsement.

## Reading the comparisons

The reports connect specialist experience to identified CoreDoc rules. Some
passages support a rule's rationale; others illustrate a risk or clarify its
scope. Several comparisons from one consultation are not independent evidence.
Each report keeps its complete questions and answers, including the boundaries
of the participant's experience. Translations are identified explicitly.

The comparisons are analytical interpretations, with independent validation
pending. They are not certifications of the institutions described or specialist
sign-off on the entire FinKore contract. Any proposed financial behavior follows
the existing specification-first contribution process.
