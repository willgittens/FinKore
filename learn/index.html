The person behind FinKore

Meet the Founder

Will Gittens

Founder & Lead Maintainer · FinKore

With over 25 years in software engineering, Will Gittens has built and led software projects across international teams, working extensively with complex financial systems, approval workflows, expense management, and enterprise applications.

His experience in financial software reinforced a belief: the most important engineering decisions should never be hidden inside implementation details. They should be explicit, testable, and open to scrutiny.

That belief led him to create FinKore.

As Founder and Lead Maintainer, Will is responsible for the project's architectural direction, technical consistency, and long-term vision. His ambition is to bring together engineers, architects, and domain specialists who can challenge assumptions, contribute their expertise, and collectively raise the standards of financial software engineering.

He believes the most valuable open-source projects are not defined by how much code one person writes, but by the knowledge they bring together and the trust they earn over time.

“Great foundations are not built by one person. They are built through shared knowledge, strong principles, and the commitment to make things better.”
