FinKore Legacy Archive

The systems may change.
The lessons should not disappear.

Behind every financial system are people who learned what happens when things go wrong. Their experience carries the hidden rules, difficult decisions, and recovery lessons that source code and manuals rarely explain.

Much of that knowledge lives in personal memory. The Legacy Archive preserves it through interviews, recognizes the people who built and maintained these systems, and gives the next generation of engineers a chance to learn from them.

FinKore Experts

Financial software needs more than software engineers.

Some of the most important rules in financial software are not written by developers. They come from accounting, auditing, compliance, legal requirements, treasury operations and years of practical experience.

FinKore Experts brings these disciplines together to challenge assumptions, review financial behavior and help shape portable, verifiable specifications.

Ways to contribute

  • Review and improve specifications
  • Identify ambiguous or missing financial behavior
  • Propose new conformance scenarios
  • Share accounting, audit, legal and operational expertise
  • Participate in architectural and domain discussions

You don't need to write code to contribute. Your expertise is the contribution.

Proposed grant initiative

FinKore Angel

Support ideas. Enable progress.

Financial innovation requires more than good ideas. It requires time, technical expertise, experimentation, and the resources to turn concepts into reliable systems.

FinKore Angel is a proposed grant initiative designed to support selected fintech projects that explore, validate, integrate, or extend the FinKore foundation.

The goal is to make valuable technical work possible while producing knowledge that can benefit the wider FinKore ecosystem. The intended support is selective and primarily through grants.

Potentially supported work

  • Evaluating FinKore in real financial products
  • Developing documented integrations
  • Testing new financial scenarios
  • Creating reusable technical examples
  • Investigating specification gaps and limitations
  • Producing publicly shareable engineering findings

We believe valuable engineering deserves support, even when its most important outcome is discovering what needs to improve.

FinKore Network / Expert Review Program

Before we write the rules, we ask the experts.

A permanent initiative connecting real-world expertise with open, verifiable financial knowledge.

Developers know how to implement financial rules. But who determines whether those rules actually reflect accounting principles, audit requirements, legal obligations or real financial operations?

FinKore's Expert Review Program connects software engineering with professionals who understand the financial world beyond the code.

Through open GitHub discussions, specialists can challenge assumptions, share real-world scenarios and help identify the rules that financial systems should make explicit.

One expert answer can become a better specification. One real-world exception can expose a missing requirement. One challenged assumption can prevent the same financial mistake from being repeated across implementations.

How the Expert Review Program works

Your experience becomes part of the foundation.

  1. 01

    Share your expertise

    Answer a question, describe a real-world situation, challenge an assumption or identify a missing financial control.

  2. 02

    Review the domain

    Contributions are evaluated against existing FinKore principles, financial context and other expert feedback.

  3. 03

    Define the behavior

    Validated findings may lead to CoreDoc clarifications, new specifications, conformance scenarios or documented limitations.

  4. 04

    Build with confidence

    Portable specifications help developers implement consistent financial behavior across languages and platforms.

Expert contributions inform FinKore's specification process. Participation does not automatically constitute normative approval, certification or regulatory endorsement.

FinKore Angel

Backing the work.
Not buying the outcome.

In development

No funding is currently available. A future program depends on funding partners, available resources, and published program terms.

FinKore Angel is an initiative being developed to explore financial support for selected fintech projects working with the FinKore foundation.

We are exploring collaboration with investment funds and potential funding partners to make a future grants program possible.

Our intended model is based on grants, not payment for favorable technical conclusions.

Selected projects would be expected to define meaningful technical objectives, document their work, and share appropriate findings with the community.

An unsuccessful integration, an unexpected limitation, or a newly discovered specification gap may provide as much value as a successful implementation.

01 / Relevance

Selective support

Funding would be considered individually, based on technical relevance, feasibility, and expected value to the FinKore ecosystem.

02 / Independence

Independent results

Financial support must never require a favorable evaluation or recommendation of FinKore.

03 / Shared knowledge

Documented learning

Supported projects would document their objectives, methods, results, and limitations, and share appropriate engineering findings with the community, including findings that challenge FinKore.
